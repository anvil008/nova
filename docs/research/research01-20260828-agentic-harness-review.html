The harness is in good mechanical shape — Go build/vet/test (incl. -race), 22 hook tests, 79 skill tests and docs_check all pass, there is no command injection in Go or Python, and no OS/cache junk is tracked. The issues are in what the gates actually guarantee rather than in whether they run: tdd-guard accepts any failing-then-passing command as red→green, build-guard fails open without jq and is bypassable in a dozen ordinary ways while also blocking benign commands, the installers copy-or-symlink inconsistently and can shadow or delete user files, and the agents reference four skills that do not exist in the single-source tree. Parity across harnesses is strong in prose but weak in enforcement: Codex has no hooks and Antigravity has no Stop gate. Documentation (README, ADRs, CHANGELOG) has drifted from the code in several specific places.

Method

Step 1

Area split

The goal is decomposed into genuinely distinct areas — by subsystem, source type, or question. The fan-out count equals the number of real areas, never a fixed N.

Step 2

Blind fan-out

One read-only research agent per area, in parallel. Each is confined to its area and returns a strict findings envelope with evidence.

Step 3

Deterministic merge

Deduplicated by (area, source, finding). Conflicts are surfaced when a topic carries more than one position; coverage, gaps, and open questions are rolled up.

Step 4

Synthesis

The primary agent owns the conclusion and recommendations. Evidence from the areas is cited, never inherited as a verdict.

●

Research output is evidence, not authority. The primary agent running the research owns the consolidated conclusion.
